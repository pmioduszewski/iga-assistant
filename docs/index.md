Iga is an open-source personal AI assistant that you run on your own computer.
It keeps a long-term memory, works inside the AI coding harness you already use,
and can help with mail, calendar, tasks and habits through the accounts you
connect.

- Source code and documentation: <https://github.com/pmioduszewski/iga-assistant>
- [Privacy policy](privacy)
- [Terms of use](terms)

## Why you are seeing this page

Iga's mail skill talks to Gmail through a Google Cloud project that **each user
creates for themselves**. Google asks every such project for a home page, a
privacy policy and terms of use. This site provides those three pages.

There is no hosted Iga service. Nobody operates Iga on your behalf, and the
project maintainers never receive your mail, your tokens or any other data.
